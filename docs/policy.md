## Cancellation Policy for Classes

To open up space for your fellow students, we require that you cancel your reservation before the applicable cancellation window closes, no exceptions. This policy applies to all studio class reservations, whether at a corporate or franchise locations using membership or class package.

Studios in New York, including Long Island: In order to avoid a fee, you must cancel your reservation at least 10 hours before class starts. A $15 fee applies to late cancellations made after the cancellation window closes, and a $25 fee applies to no-shows.

All other studios: In order to avoid a fee, you must cancel your reservation at least 2 hours before class starts. A $15 fee applies to both late cancellations and no-shows.

If you booked your studio class using a class package, you will lose the value of that class credit instead of being charged the late cancel or no-show fee. A reservation cancelled within the applicable window (2 hours, or 10 hours for New York studios) will result in either the fee described above or loss of the class credit, depending on how you paid.


## Membership Options
We offer two membership choices to meet you where you are in your practice.

All Access Membership gives you unlimited access to in-studio classes. It offers unlimited classes at any studio across the United States.

Alternatively, you can purchase single classes.

## Membership Cancellation Policy
You can cancel anytime with an early termination fee that is equal to 1-month (6-month membership) or 2 months (12-month membership).

You will be able to enjoy all your membership benefits up until your practice until date.

Please note you will not be able to reinstate your membership but will need to restart a new committed membership if you decide to rejoin.

You also have the option to freeze your membership for up to 30 days for no fee at all.
